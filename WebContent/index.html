About Skill Share Hub

Skill Share Hub is a platform where students can exchange knowledge and skills with each other and learn from each other.

Features

  • Find people with useful skills
  • Share your own skills and knowledge
  • Search for learning opportunities
  • Send skill exchange requests and offers

How It Works

  1. Sign up and create your profile.
  2. Add your skills and expertise.
  3. Search for skills you want to learn.
  4. Connect with others and start learning together!
